# Privacy Policy: NoteKar

NoteKar operates on an **offline-first and privacy-focused architecture**.

- 🛡️ **No Tracking & Analytics SDKs:** No third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs are embedded in the app or website.
- 💾 **Local Data Storage:** All user moments, notes, tags, and settings are stored locally on your device (using Isar and `SharedPreferences` in the Android app, and IndexedDB and `localStorage` in the Web PWA).
- 🔒 **No Account or Cloud Log Sync:** Core time logging and journal entries remain strictly on your device. We do not operate remote databases storing your entries.
- 📡 **Transparent Network Access:**
  - The Android app and Web PWA contact public GitHub endpoints strictly for software update checks, release notes, and bulletin feeds.
  - No user-authored notes, timestamps, tags, or personal profiles are ever transmitted in these requests.
  - The public website and web app are hosted via Vercel.
- 📲 **System Backups:** Optional Android OS system backup (e.g., Google Drive) is managed directly by the Android operating system if enabled by the user.
