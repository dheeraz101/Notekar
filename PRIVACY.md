# Privacy Policy: NoteKar

NoteKar operates on a strict **privacy-first and offline-first model**.

- 🛡️ **Zero Tracking & Analytics:** We do not collect, aggregate, or sell user data.
- 💾 **Local Data Storage:** All timestamps and notes are stored locally in your browser via IndexedDB and LocalStorage.
- 📱 **Android App Integration:** NoteKar Android App stores data locally via Hive and supports standard Android OS Google Drive system backups.
- 📡 **Minimal Permissions:** Internet permission is used exclusively for checking software updates and bug announcements.
